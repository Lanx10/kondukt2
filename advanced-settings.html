APPEARANCE

Applies the moment you tap it. There is nothing to save.

Installs over Kondukt 1.0.0, the version on this handset. Everything saved here stays.

HOW THIS SAVES

  • Takes effect immediately. The whole screen changes as you tap, so you see the mode before you decide to keep it.

  • Stays on the handset. It is written to local storage with the rest of the configuration. Nothing is uploaded.

  • Is read once at launch. Kondukt opens in the mode you leave it in, so a change made at night is still there at first boarding.

OFFLINE STORAGE

All configurations are saved locally and work offline.
